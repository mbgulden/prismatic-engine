# Welcome to the Prismatic Swarm

We are excited to announce the launch of the **Prismatic Agent Hub** (codenamed **Prism**). 

This hub acts as a central coordinator for our heterogeneous fleet of AI workers. By splitting incoming tasks into specialized wavelengths, we achieve full-spectrum autonomy.

## Key Features
- **Hub-and-Spoke Design**: Central coordinator + specialized agents.
- **Bolt-on Skills**: Easily extendable capabilities.
- **Platform Agnostic**: Works with Hermes, Open Claw, and more.
