# The Power of Antigravity (AGY)

When it comes to processing massive amounts of information, **Antigravity (AGY)** is our go-to agent.

With a **1M-token context window**, AGY can ingest entire codebases, 500-page PDFs, or complex research corpora in a single session.

## Use Cases
- **Competitive Research**: Analyzing dozens of competitor sites and strategy papers.
- **Visual QA**: Comparing screenshots and design mockups with pixel precision.
- **Knowledge Synthesis**: Creating summary reports from years of archived logs.

AGY transforms "Too Long; Didn't Read" into actionable intelligence.
